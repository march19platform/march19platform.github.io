March 19 Platform is a democratic movement advocating for justice, human rights, and democracy in Turkey.

## Our Mission

We believe in the power of grassroots organizing, community solidarity, and peaceful advocacy to create meaningful change. Our platform serves as a hub for:

- **Information sharing** through our weekly Fanzin publication
- **Community building** via panel discussions and events
- **Documentation** of human rights issues and democratic movements
- **Solidarity** with organizations aligned with our values

## Our History

March 19 Platform was established to provide a voice for those advocating for democratic values and human rights. Through our publications, events, and campaigns, we work to raise awareness and build connections within the diaspora community.

## Get Involved

There are many ways to support our mission:

- Subscribe to our newsletter for updates
- Share our Fanzin with your community
- Attend our panel discussions and events
- Follow us on social media
