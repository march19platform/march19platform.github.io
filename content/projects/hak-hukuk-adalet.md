The "Hak Hukuk Adalet" (Rights, Law, Justice) exhibition is a powerful visual documentation of protests and demonstrations that took place across 32 cities.

## About the Exhibition

This traveling exhibition features photographs capturing moments of solidarity, courage, and hope from democratic movements. The images tell the stories of ordinary people standing up for their rights.

## Exhibition History

The exhibition has traveled to multiple cities, raising awareness and fostering dialogue about human rights and democratic values.
