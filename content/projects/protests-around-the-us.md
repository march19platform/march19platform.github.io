In line with our commitment to democratic participation, we coordinate and support peaceful demonstrations following the politically motivated prosecution and detention of Istanbul's elected mayor, Ekrem İmamoğlu. Each gathering is designed to:

**Defend the ballot box.** We stand against judicial tactics that overturn the will of voters.

**Amplify silenced voices.** By gathering in public, we draw national and international attention to shrinking civic space in Türkiye.

**Model constructive dissent.** Every action follows strict non-violent guidelines and welcomes people of all backgrounds, reflecting our inclusive, nonpartisan ethos.

**Build community.** Local organizers pair each protest with teach-ins or letter-writing sessions, turning outrage into sustained civic engagement.

## Where we mobilize

We have organized or joined protests in the following U.S. cities, with new locations added as local chapters form:

## How to take part

**Find a city lead.** Check our events calendar or contact us.

**Show up, speak up.** Bring a friend, a sign, and a commitment to peaceful assembly.

Together we demonstrate that democratic rights neither begin nor end at national borders, and that the struggle for an open, pluralistic Türkiye will be won by citizens everywhere who refuse to stay silent.
